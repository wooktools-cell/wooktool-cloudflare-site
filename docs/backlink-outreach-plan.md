# wooktools Backlink and Off-Site Authority Plan

Date: 2026-07-11

## Purpose

Build external authority, referral traffic, and brand trust for wooktools without using risky link schemes.

This plan supports:

- Faster discovery of new SEO/GEO pages.
- Better trust signals for B2B sourcing keywords.
- Referral traffic from buyers, distributors, sourcing teams, and industry researchers.
- Safer long-term growth for Google Search and AI search surfaces.

## Policy Basis

This plan follows these Google principles:

- Avoid link spam and artificial ranking manipulation.
- Create helpful, reliable, people-first content.
- Build non-commodity content with original value, especially for AI search and long-tail sourcing questions.

Reference:

- Google spam policies: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Google helpful content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google AI search optimization guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## What We Will Not Do

Do not use:

- Bulk paid backlink packages.
- Private blog networks.
- Auto-generated guest posts.
- Comment spam.
- Forum profile spam.
- Exact-match anchor text at scale.
- Hidden links.
- Low-quality article directories.
- Expired-domain link schemes.
- Third-party pages placed only to exploit another site's authority.

If a placement is paid, sponsored, affiliate, or advertorial, the link should be marked appropriately by the publisher.

## Link Prospect Scoring

Before outreach, score each website.

| Criterion | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Topical relevance | Unrelated | General business | Tools, sourcing, hardware, manufacturing, import/export |
| Audience relevance | Consumers only | Mixed | Importers, distributors, wholesalers, procurement teams |
| Page quality | Thin/spammy | Acceptable | Real editorial or useful resource |
| Link reason | No natural reason | Weak reason | Clear reason to cite wooktools content |
| Risk | Obvious spam | Unknown | Safe and transparent |

Minimum outreach threshold: 7/10.

Reject immediately if:

- The site sells bulk dofollow links.
- The page is unrelated to tools, sourcing, manufacturing, or B2B trade.
- The site has excessive outbound links to unrelated niches.
- The publisher demands exact-match anchor text.
- The content offers no real buyer value.

## Anchor Text Rules

Use natural anchor text. Avoid over-optimized exact-match repetition.

Recommended anchors:

- wooktools
- WOOKTOOL
- wooktools tape measure guide
- tape measure manufacturer guide
- custom tape measure supplier page
- tape measure quality control checklist
- WOOKTOOL factory information
- hand tool sourcing checklist

Avoid repeating anchors such as:

- custom tape measure manufacturer
- tape measure manufacturer China
- OEM tape measure China

Exact-match anchors can appear occasionally, but they should not dominate the backlink profile.

## 12-Week Execution Plan

### Weeks 1-2: Foundation and owned profiles

Tasks:

1. Audit all existing owned profiles.
2. Ensure company name, brand name, website, email, and description are consistent.
3. Add links to the most relevant pages, not only homepage.
4. Publish 3-5 LinkedIn/Facebook posts pointing to useful guides.
5. Create an outreach tracking spreadsheet.

Profile text standard:

`wooktools is a China-based custom hand tools manufacturer supplying WOOKTOOL tape measures, utility knives, screwdriver sets, OEM/ODM tools, and private-label packaging for global B2B buyers.`

Target links:

- Homepage: `/`
- Product overview: `/products/`
- Custom tape measure: `/custom-tape-measure/`
- Factory: `/factory/`
- Contact: `/contact/`

### Weeks 3-4: Directory and citation cleanup

Tasks:

1. Submit or update legitimate B2B manufacturer profiles.
2. Use consistent categories:
   - Hand tools manufacturer
   - Tape measure manufacturer
   - Utility knife manufacturer
   - Screwdriver set manufacturer
   - OEM/ODM hand tools supplier
3. Add real factory/product photos when the platform allows it.
4. Avoid duplicate thin descriptions.

Goal:

- 10-20 clean citations or profile links.
- At least 5 profiles with product/category-specific links.

### Weeks 5-8: Editorial outreach

Promote useful assets instead of asking for generic homepage links.

Recommended outreach angles:

### Weeks 9-12: Guest expert content and partner mentions

Tasks:

2. Ask real business partners for supplier/resource mentions.
3. Publish short LinkedIn posts summarizing new guides.
4. Add one downloadable buyer checklist if needed.

Goal:
