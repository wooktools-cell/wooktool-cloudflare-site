# WOOKTOOL Blog Content Standard

Updated: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

WOOKTOOL blog content should help real B2B buyers make sourcing decisions. The goal is not to publish many keyword pages. The goal is to publish useful sourcing articles with real product context, factory experience, trustworthy data, practical checklists, and clear RFQ paths.

This standard applies to every new blog article.

## Core Rules

1. Write for a real buyer first.
2. Use one primary search intent per article.
3. Add useful experience from WOOKTOOL production, QC, packaging, or RFQ handling.
4. Use real product photos or factory photos when they add proof.
5. Use public data only when it supports the buyer's decision.
6. Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.
7. Link to the most relevant category, product, factory, quality, OEM/ODM, and RFQ pages.
8. End with a practical next step, not a generic sales paragraph.

## Source and Data Rules

Every industry-analysis article should include at least one of these evidence types:

- Public industry data from credible sources.
- Trade policy, import/export, standards, or market information from official or well-known sources.
- WOOKTOOL factory experience, such as common RFQ questions, sample issues, QC checkpoints, packaging decisions, or buyer scenarios.
- Product-level observations from real photos, specifications, test reports, packaging, or production steps.

When using external data:

- Cite the source in natural language.
- Use the data to explain a buying decision.
- Avoid copying long passages.
- Do not use old market data unless the article clearly says why it still matters.
- Re-check current statistics before publishing if the article says "current", "latest", "recent", "today", or names a specific year.

Preferred data source types:

- Official government customs, trade, tariff, or standards sources.
- Google Search Central for SEO/content standards.
- Standards organizations and certification/testing references where relevant.
- Industry associations, public market reports, and reputable B2B platform trend pages.
- WOOKTOOL internal production, QC, packaging, and RFQ observations.

Avoid:

- Unsourced market-size claims.
- Fake statistics.
- Vague phrases such as "the market is growing rapidly" without context.
- Using data only to make the article look longer.

## Product Photo Rules

Use real WOOKTOOL product or factory images whenever possible.

Photo requirements:

- Product images should be clear and relevant to the article section.
- Use 1000 x 1000 product images where practical for product examples.
- Use factory/process images for manufacturing, QC, packing, injection molding, assembly, and certification topics.
- Use descriptive filenames, such as `tape-measure-hook-options.png`, not generic names such as `1.jpg`.
- Use descriptive alt text that explains the image naturally.
- Do not stuff alt text with repeated keywords.
- Place images near the paragraph they support.
- Keep image count reasonable so the page remains fast.

Recommended article image pattern:

- 1 main/featured image.
- 2 to 4 supporting images for product examples, QC points, packaging, or factory proof.
- Use captions when an image shows a specific buyer decision or inspection point.

## Keyword Use Rules

Use keywords as a guide, not as filler.

Allowed:

- Use the primary keyword in the title when natural.
- Use related terms in headings only when they help the reader scan the article.
- Use product names, buyer types, and sourcing situations naturally.
- Use exact-match keywords only a few times if they fit the sentence.

Avoid:

- Repeating the same phrase in every heading.
- Lists of keyword variations.
- City/country/product keyword blocks.
- Awkward sentences written only for search engines.
- Unnatural alt text or anchor text.

Example:

- Good: "For screwdriver set buyers, the most common inspection problems are missing bits, weak magnetic tips, and poor case layout."
- Bad: "Screwdriver set manufacturer China screwdriver set supplier China OEM screwdriver set factory provides screwdriver set China."

## Article Structure

Use this structure for most new articles:

1. Buyer problem: Name the real sourcing problem.
2. Industry or market context: Add one useful data point, trend, regulation, buyer behavior, or supply-chain observation.
3. Product/factory evidence: Use real product examples, factory process photos, QC experience, or packaging examples.
4. Decision framework: Explain how the buyer should choose or evaluate options.
5. Common mistakes: Show risks that cause returns, delays, or poor retail performance.
6. Practical checklist: Give buyers a usable list for RFQ, sample review, inspection, or packaging confirmation.
7. Internal links: Link to the matching WOOKTOOL landing pages.
8. RFQ next step: Tell buyers what to send.

## Required Internal Links

Each article must include:

- 1 link to the main commercial landing page.
- 1 link to `/contact/#rfq-form`.
- 1 trust link to `/factory/`, `/quality-control/`, or `/oem-odm/`.
- 1 to 3 product detail links if specific products are mentioned.

Anchor text should describe the destination.

Good anchors:

- custom tape measure supplier
- WOOKTOOL factory page
- hand tools quality control process
- send a product RFQ

Weak anchors:

- click here
- more
- this page
- link

## Article Types

### Product Sourcing Guide

Use for topics such as tape measures, utility knives, screwdriver sets, replacement blades, and custom packaging.

Must include:

- Product types.
- Buyer channels.
- Specifications to confirm.
- Packaging options.
- QC points.
- RFQ checklist.
- Product/category images.

### Industry Analysis

Use for broader market or buyer-behavior topics.

Must include:

- A real trend or data point.
- What it means for buyers.
- How product selection, packaging, MOQ, or QC should change.
- One practical recommendation table or checklist.

### Quality / Inspection Guide

Use for defects, inspection, testing, certificates, and pre-shipment topics.

Must include:

- Real inspection checkpoints.
- Common defect examples.
- Product or factory photos.
- How buyers can reduce risk.
- Link to `/quality-control/` and `/factory/`.

### Customization / OEM Guide

Use for logo, color, packaging, molds, samples, barcode, and private-label topics.

Must include:

- What can be customized.
- What affects cost and MOQ.
- What buyers should prepare before RFQ.
- What sample approval should check.
- Link to `/oem-odm/`, relevant product/category page, and `/contact/#rfq-form`.

## Publishing Checklist

Before publishing each article:

- Title is clear and not exaggerated.
- Description is under 165 characters where practical.
- Article has one main search intent.
- At least one useful data point, production observation, or real product/factory example is included.
- Real images are used when relevant.
- Image filenames and alt text are descriptive.
- No keyword stuffing.
- Internal links are present and useful.
- RFQ next step is clear.
- Build passes.
- Sitemap includes the article.
- Links return 200 and do not create 404s.
