# wooktools SEO, GEO, CRO, Backlink, and Social Growth Plan

Date: 2026-09-01

## Current Stage

The priority pages have been indexed. The next stage is not simply publishing more pages. The goal is to build ranking strength, buyer trust, and RFQ conversion around pages that already have a chance to appear in Google.

## Main Objective

Increase qualified search visibility and RFQ opportunities for wooktools / WOOKTOOL by combining:

- Content depth for commercial long-tail keywords.
- Conversion-focused page paths from guide to product to RFQ.
- External authority from relevant directories, partners, and sourcing resources.
- Social distribution that brings visitors back to useful website pages.
- GEO-ready concise answers for AI search and answer engines.

## 90-Day Execution Tracks

### Track 1: Content That Can Rank

Frequency: 2 high-quality articles or resources per week.

Priority topics:

1. Tape measure RFQ and quality-control checklist.
2. Utility knife blade and safety selection guide.
3. Private-label packaging checklist for hand tools.
4. CE and RoHS documents for tape measure buyers.
5. Inside a tape measure factory: injection, assembly, QC, packing.
6. Screwdriver set inspection checklist for importers.
7. Hand tool product data checklist for distributors.
8. MOQ and sample approval guide for custom hand tools.

Each content page must include:

- One buyer problem.
- One practical table or checklist.
- Real product or factory evidence when relevant.
- Internal links to product/category pages.
- RFQ path to `/contact/#rfq-form`.
- FAQ or concise answer block where appropriate.

### Track 3: External Authority

Do not chase link volume. Build links only where the page has a real reason to reference wooktools.

Primary assets to promote:

- `/resources/tape-measure-rfq-quality-checklist/`
- `/buyer-guides/`
- `/blog/tape-measure-quality-defects/`
- `/blog/choose-tape-measure-manufacturer/`
- `/factory/`
- `/quality-control/`

First 30 outreach targets:

- 10 B2B supplier directories.
- 8 sourcing/import blogs.
- 5 packaging or inspection resource pages.
- 5 hardware/tool industry resource pages.
- 2 partner or supplier mention opportunities.

Outreach angle:

- Promote practical sourcing checklists and factory/QC resources.
- Avoid asking for generic homepage links.
- Use natural brand anchors such as `wooktools`, `WOOKTOOL`, and `tape measure RFQ checklist`.

### Track 4: Social Distribution

Recommended weekly rhythm:

- 2 LinkedIn posts.
- 2 Facebook posts.
- 1 short factory/product process post.
- 1 Alibaba feed/product update when relevant.

Post types:

1. Checklist summary post linking to a buyer guide.
2. Product photo post linking to a product/category page.
3. Factory/QC trust post linking to `/factory/` or `/quality-control/`.
4. Short opinion post linking to an industry analysis article.

Tracking:

- Add UTM links for social posts.
- Monitor GA4 traffic source, engaged sessions, and lead events.
- Track which posts create website clicks, not only likes.
