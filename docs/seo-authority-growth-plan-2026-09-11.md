# WOOKTOOL SEO and Authority Growth Plan — 2026-09-11

## Objective

Increase qualified B2B impressions, organic clicks, referral visits, and RFQ starts without buying links, creating deceptive reviews, or publishing inaccurate price claims.

## Priority 1 — Fix Google eligibility signals

- Keep `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`, canonical URLs, sitemap entries, image alt text, and visible product specifications on all buyer-facing pages.
- Only publish Google `Product` rich-result markup when a product has a public, verifiable offer with price, currency, and availability. Quote-only OEM pages should use buyer content, FAQs, and RFQ calls to action instead of incomplete product markup.
- In Search Console, validate the Product snippets fix after Google recrawls. Do not add placeholder prices, aggregate ratings, or reviews.

## Priority 3 — Content sequence for the next 60 days

1. **Tape Measure Specification Sheet Template for Importers** — a downloadable-style page with a copyable table for length, blade width, hook, unit layout, tolerance, packaging, barcode, and carton mark. This is a useful external-link target.
2. **Magnetic Tape Measure vs Standard Hook: Buyer Selection Guide** — covers hook use case, pull strength, rivets, coating, and sample checks. Link to the heavy-duty and 10m/33ft product directions.
3. **How to Read Imperial Tape Measure Fractions for Hardware Buyers** — targets metric-to-imperial sourcing questions and links naturally to dual-unit blade products.
4. **Tape Measure Packaging Guide: Blister Card, Color Box, Display Box, and Carton Mark** — supports private-label and distributor inquiries.

Each new page should answer one distinct buyer question, include a decision table, use only genuine product/factory images, link to one primary category and one RFQ resource, and contain one non-repeated call to action.

## Priority 4 — Fast, safe authority acquisition

1. Publish the tape measure RFQ checklist and steel tape measure guide from the owned LinkedIn and Facebook profiles with UTM links.
2. Update the Alibaba company/profile destination and relevant product descriptions with the appropriate WOOKTOOL category or buyer-resource link where the platform permits it.
3. Build listings on legitimate manufacturer, trade-association, chamber, and industrial directory profiles that allow a company website link. Keep company name, site URL, business email, and product categories consistent.
4. Offer the RFQ checklist to inspection partners, sourcing consultants, hardware distributor blogs, and trade-publication resource pages as a practical reference. Pitch the resource page rather than a sales page.
5. Request supplier/customer case-study mentions only where the relationship is real and the content can describe a genuine product program or sourcing process.

Avoid paid link farms, mass guest-post packages, automated comments, reciprocal-link pages with no relevance, copied articles, and fabricated review/rating schema. These can create short-lived link counts but weaken trust and add manual-action risk.

## Weekly measurement

- Search Console: impressions, clicks, average position, indexed pages, product snippet issues, and queries containing `tape measure`, `custom`, `private label`, `OEM`, `supplier`, and `manufacturer`.
- GA4: visits from `linkedin`, `facebook`, `alibaba`, `outreach`, and `referral`; RFQ start and submit events; WhatsApp and email clicks.
- Outreach: contacts sent, replies, live referring domains, referred sessions, and RFQs attributed to referral traffic.

Review titles and descriptions only after enough impression data has accumulated; change one field at a time and keep an annotation of the date and page changed.
