## Short answer

Low MOQ custom hand tools are possible when buyers start from existing models and keep customization focused. Logo printing, packaging artwork, and standard color options are usually easier than new molds, special materials, or fully custom structures.

## What affects MOQ

| Custom request | MOQ impact | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Existing model with logo | Lower | Uses current components |
| Standard color with package artwork | Lower to medium | Packaging setup may still apply |
| Custom shell or handle color | Medium | Material color batch may be required |
| Special blade printing | Medium to high | Printing setup and minimum run affect cost |
| New mold | High | Tooling investment and testing are required |

## How small buyers should prepare

Small buyers should avoid starting with too many custom requirements at once. A practical first order may use an existing WOOKTOOL product, buyer logo, simple packaging adjustment, and clear quality inspection.

## FAQ

### Is low MOQ possible for custom packaging?

It depends on packaging type, artwork, printing method, and supplier setup. Simple labels or existing packaging structures are usually easier.

### Does a new mold work for low MOQ?

Usually no. New mold projects need higher volume to justify tooling, testing, and production setup.

### What should small buyers send first?

Send product reference photos, logo file, expected quantity, target market, packaging idea, and target price if available.
