## Short answer

To source hand tools from China, buyers should define the product specification before asking for price. The supplier needs to know the tool type, material, size, function, logo requirement, packaging style, estimated quantity, target market, and quality-control expectation.

## Sourcing checklist

| Step | Buyer action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Define product | Send product type and reference photo | Avoids quoting the wrong model |
| Confirm market | Retail, wholesale, industrial, promotion, private label | Affects quality level and packaging |
| Review customization | Logo, color, blade, handle, packaging | Affects MOQ and sample timing |
| Confirm sample | Sample before bulk production | Reduces mismatch risk |
| Set QC standard | Function, appearance, packaging, carton | Reduces claims after shipment |
| Plan delivery | Sample approval and shipment schedule | Keeps launch timing realistic |

## FAQ

### Is the cheapest quote always the best starting point?

No. The cheapest quote may reduce material, packaging, inspection scope, or durability. Buyers should compare specifications, not only unit price.

### Should buyers send a target price?

Yes, if possible. A target price helps the factory recommend a realistic model and avoid quoting a product that does not fit the market.
