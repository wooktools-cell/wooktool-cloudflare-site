## Short answer

Private label hand tools are tools produced by a manufacturer and sold under a buyer's own brand. For B2B buyers, this usually means choosing an existing product model, adding a custom logo, selecting product colors, confirming packaging artwork, and setting quality-control requirements before bulk production.

wooktools supports private-label programs for WOOKTOOL tape measures, utility knives, screwdriver sets, replacement blades, and selected OEM/ODM hand tool projects.

## What private label usually includes

Private label does not always require a fully new product design. Many importers and distributors start from a proven factory model, then customize the commercial details that buyers see in the retail channel.

| Private label item | Common options | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Case printing, label, package artwork | Builds brand recognition |
| Product color | Shell color, handle color, accent color | Matches buyer brand or retail line |
| Packaging | Blister card, color box, display box, bulk carton | Affects shelf display and shipping cost |
| Barcode and labels | SKU, barcode, warning text, carton mark | Needed for retail and warehouse handling |
| Quality standard | Function, appearance, packaging, carton strength | Reduces complaints and repacking work |

## Buyer checklist before RFQ

- Product category and reference photo
- Target market and sales channel
- Logo file and brand color direction
- Estimated order quantity
- Packaging style and barcode needs
- Quality expectation and inspection points
- Target shipment schedule

## When private label is better than full custom development

Private label is often better when buyers want a faster launch, lower development risk, or a smaller first order. A fully custom mold may be useful later, but existing WOOKTOOL models can help buyers test a product line before investing in new tooling.

## FAQ

### Is private label the same as OEM?

Private label can be part of OEM, but it usually focuses on branding and packaging for existing or lightly customized products.
