## Short answer

Hand tool packaging should match the buyer's sales channel. Bulk packaging is efficient for wholesalers, blister cards and color boxes are useful for retail stores, and display boxes can support counter display. Private-label buyers should confirm package artwork, barcode, warning text, carton marks, and product protection before bulk production.

## Packaging comparison

| Packaging type | Best for | Advantages | Watch point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulk packaging | Wholesalers, industrial supply | Lower cost and simple handling | Less retail presentation |
| Blister card | Hardware stores, retail shelves | Product visibility and barcode-ready display | Artwork and card strength |
| Color box | Private label brands | Better brand presentation | Box size and shipping volume |
| Display box | Counter display | Easy retail promotion | Inner packing and carton protection |

## What buyers should send

- Product type and quantity
- Sales channel
- Packaging reference photo

## FAQ

### Does packaging affect MOQ?

Yes. Custom artwork, printing method, box structure, barcode labels, and display packaging can affect MOQ and sample time.

### Can one product use different packaging for different markets?

Yes. The same product model may use bulk packing for wholesalers and blister card or color box for retail buyers.

### Should packaging be inspected before shipment?

Yes. Packaging inspection should include product placement, barcode, labels, warning text, artwork, carton marks, and carton strength.
